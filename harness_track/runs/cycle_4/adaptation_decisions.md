# Adaptation decisions — what carries over from Part 1

One row per component. "Carry over" is one of: AS-IS (used unchanged), ADAPTED (method kept, parameters or inputs changed), RE-ESTIMATED (method kept, values re-fitted on emerging data), BORROWED (taken from the developed market because emerging data cannot support it), DROPPED. Evidence names the file or table that supports the decision. Remaining uncertainty says what could still be wrong and, where known, how much the predicted level moves if it is.

## What changed in this market (summary)

The emerging book is sold through brokers (70% vs 25%), to younger and less experienced customers (young segments 47% vs 28%; median years licensed 24 vs 28), with older vehicles (no car under 3 years) and more renters (43% vs 30%); it records age only in bands and has no credit, mileage or telematics data, but has gender. The climate is warmer and wetter with twice the hail: motor claims are 30% more frequent and 45% smaller (glass/hail and third-party property up, bodily-injury share down), buildings frequency is unchanged but the cost is dominated by a handful of fire claims (45% of cost vs 23%), and contents claims are 15% less frequent at the same severity. Expense loadings are heavier (fixed expense 85 vs 45 CU, broker commission 20% vs 15%, cat reinsurance 12% vs 6%) and premium tax lighter (8% vs 12%). The 2025 test year is 76% new business from a transferred broker portfolio with the same channel and region mix as the renewals but a younger age mix; in training, new business runs 17–25% worse than renewals after age standardisation. The 2023 training year is a survivor sample (100% persistence, no lapses).

## Open decisions for the team

1. **Buildings level.** 2023 completed burning cost 515, 2024 324; fire is 55% / 30% of cost. Choose the basis for 2025 (two-year average ≈ 417 × trend ≈ 458, 2024 alone ≈ 356, or capped with the developed fire share) — this is a ±30% decision on the line.
2. **Trend scenario.** Apply the developed-market trend (central) or none (alternative); both candidate files are produced (R-0505).
3. **New-business relativity.** Confirm that the 2025 broker book should be priced at the training new-business relativity (+17–25% frequency, age-standardised) rather than at the renewal level or at a transferred-book loading from the broker's own experience.
4. **F-06 pre-inception claims** (36, 0.33%): confirm exclusion or supply corrected loss dates.
5. **F-21 `declined_by_insurer`**: confirm what the value means; it does not end policies.
6. **F-22 comparison-site commission** for the emerging market is null: supply before any premium build.
7. **F-23 `reference_policies`**: state its purpose (rate check? reference quotes?).
8. **`vehicle_age` definition**: confirm whether age is measured the same way as in the developed market (no emerging vehicle is under 3 years).
9. **Gender** is recorded in this market: confirm whether it may be used as a rating factor before any model that includes it is adopted.
10. **Survivor sample 2023**: accept that the 2023 → 2024 validation fold trains on a renewal-conditioned book, or restrict the fold to 2024 policies also present in 2023.
